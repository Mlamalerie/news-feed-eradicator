# Credits and notices

## Original project

Silent Feed is a fork of **[News Feed Eradicator](https://github.com/jordwest/news-feed-eradicator)**, created by **[Jordan West](https://west.io/)** in 2012 and developed since with many contributors ([list of contributors](https://github.com/jordwest/news-feed-eradicator/graphs/contributors)). The site-blocking engine, the options page, the quote widget, the snooze feature and most of the supported sites come from that project. Thank you to Jordan West and to everyone who contributed.

## What this fork adds

By Mlamali Said Salimo: the YouTube music filter and its settings, Shorts hiding, TikTok support, Instagram Reels, French quotes, the statistics page, site logos and the new icon.
