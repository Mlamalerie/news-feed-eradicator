# Privacy policy — Stop Scrolling, Start Winning

*Last updated: 1 November 2026*

Stop Scrolling, Start Winning is a browser extension made by Mlamali Said Salimo ("Mlamali The Engineer"). It does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores

Everything stays in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your own device:

- your settings (which sites are switched on, hidden regions, music filter, quotes, style);
- your blocks (schedules and time limits);
- the time you spend on the sites you switched on, per hour, kept for 14 days, so that daily and hourly limits work;
- counts of what was hidden (music videos, replaced feeds), kept for 90 days, for the Statistics tab.

To decide what to hide, the extension reads the text of the pages of the sites you switched on (for example a video title or channel name). This happens on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

## What the extension does not do

- It sends no data to the developer or to any third party. It has no server, no analytics and no tracking.
- It does not read pages on sites you have not switched on: it only asks for access to a supported site when you switch that site on.
- It does not run remote code.

## Deleting your data

Statistics can be reset from the Statistics tab. Removing the extension deletes all of its data.
